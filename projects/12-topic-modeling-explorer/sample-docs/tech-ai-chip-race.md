---
title: AI Chip Race Reshapes Cloud Spending
source: BBC-like Technology
category: technology
---

Cloud providers are racing to secure graphics processors for generative AI services. Analysts say the cost of training large language models has moved from software budgets into infrastructure planning. Startups are signing multi-year compute contracts, while established vendors are designing custom accelerators to reduce energy use. The shift has made chip supply, data center cooling and model efficiency central issues for technology executives.
