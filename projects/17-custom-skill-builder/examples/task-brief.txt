Name: Research Brief Summarizer
Task: Convert long research notes into concise structured briefings for AI agents.
Trigger: Use when the user provides notes, links, or pasted research and wants a reusable agent workflow for summarization.
Inputs: research notes, source URLs, target audience, desired depth
Outputs: executive summary, key evidence, open questions, next actions
Constraints: do not invent facts, preserve source attribution, keep recommendations separate from evidence
